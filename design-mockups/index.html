Portfolio Design Mockups

Eleven static explorations of the portfolio UI. Same content as the live site — different visual treatments. 1–3 stay close to the original; 4–6 go wild; 7–10 riff on the Terminal; 11 goes 3D. Click one to view the full page.

1 · Soft Glass

Drops the hard 1px frame for a floating pill nav, rounded glassy cards with gaps, amber radial glows behind the hero, and pill-shaped tags/buttons. Same dark + amber palette, softer feel.

Rounded · Glowy · Modern SaaS

2 · Light Editorial

Keeps the signature frame lines and tiled 1px project grid, but flips to a warm paper background with serif display headings, numbered sections, and underline-style form fields.

Paper · Serif · Magazine

3 · Midnight Gradient

Closest to the current site: keeps the frame, marquee, and layout, but shifts to a blue-tinted dark palette and leans into the multi-color gradient — gradient nav line, always-on card edges, gradient CTAs.

Frame kept · Gradient-forward

4 · Terminal

Full CRT hacker aesthetic: green phosphor monospace, scanlines and flicker, ASCII-art name, projects as terminal file windows (screenshots tinted green until hover), experience as a log, and a command-prompt contact form.

Monospace · Scanlines · bidhan@uta:~$

5 · Neo-Brutalist

Loud and playful: thick black borders, hard offset shadows, giant outlined uppercase type, rotated stickers and badges, candy accent blocks, and a scrolling skills band. Everything shoves and snaps on hover.

Thick borders · Stickers · Loud

6 · Synthwave

Retro-future sunset: striped neon sun, twinkling stars, an animated perspective grid floor racing under the hero, chrome-gradient italic type, chamfered neon cards, and a glowing "Transmit" contact form.

Neon · Grid floor · 1986

7 · Amber CRT / neofetch

Warm amber phosphor instead of green. The hero is a neofetch system readout — your headshot rendered as a dithered amber "8-bit portrait" (hover to develop it into color) beside OS-style specs. Double-border TUI project boxes, a man-page for experience, and a pine mail composer.

Amber glow · neofetch · man bidhan

8 · Editor / tmux

A modern dev setup in Tokyo Night colors: editor tabs up top, a vim statusline pinned to the bottom, and a split hero — your profile as syntax-highlighted JS on the left, your headshot in an image-preview pane on the right. Projects are tmux panes, experience is a git log.

Tokyo Night · Panes · git log

9 · BIOS Boot / DOS

A PC booting up in 1994: BIOS POST lines that appear one by one ("Detecting Passion Master... MACHINE LEARNING"), a cyan ASCII banner, Norton-Commander blue project panels with F3 VIEW badges, a DOS setup dialog for contact, and a function-key bar pinned at the bottom.

POST screen · NC blue · F-keys

10 · Terminal V2

The green Terminal, upgraded: identical everywhere, plus the framed dithered portrait from the Amber mockup sitting in the hero's free right space — recolored to green phosphor, with the same hover-to-develop effect.

Terminal + phosphor portrait

11 · Dimension (3D)

Real depth everywhere: cards physically tilt toward your cursor with a moving glare, extruded 3D headline, a spinning tech cube orbiting your floating portrait card, press-down chunky buttons, and layered elements that pop off each card in 3D space.

Tilt cards · Extruded text · Spinning cube
